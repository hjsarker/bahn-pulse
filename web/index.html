Sources, definitions & limits

This is a research pilot, not a live journey planner or a forecast.

What is counted? Scheduled station arrival records for the displayed reporting month, excluding additional unscheduled stops and bus replacement services. Six-minute late rate is calculated from latest reported arrival change time minus scheduled arrival time for non-canceled arrivals. Cancellations are reported separately over scheduled arrivals. A train visiting five stations may produce five arrival records.

Source: Deutsche Bahn Data archive by Piet Brömmel, derived from DB public APIs, CC BY 4.0. The archive has collection gaps and retrospective schema revisions. This sample is not directly comparable with official DB passenger or operating punctuality statistics.

Not built yet: multi-month seasons, verified origin-to-destination journey pairs, weather relationships, construction event attribution and predictive models. Those require additional source validation and evaluation.
